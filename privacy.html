This policy explains how the developer of Pure Logic: Brain Puzzles handles information in the app and through support. Contact: abc466254511@gmail.com.

1. Data stored on your device

The app stores game progress, settings, collections, points, free-play records, and purchase entitlement caches locally to provide gameplay and restore your session. The app does not provide its own user accounts, advertising, remote product analytics, or account-based cloud synchronization.

2. Local diagnostics

The app keeps limited local operation and error events to help diagnose problems. These include event time, event type, and optional game or difficulty. They do not include board contents, puzzle seeds, user identifiers, transaction identifiers, or free-form user text. Records are limited to 300 events and seven days, are excluded from system backup, and are not uploaded by the app.

3. Apple purchases

Apple processes payments and transactions through the App Store. The app uses StoreKit to verify transactions and determine purchase entitlements. The developer does not receive your full payment card details. Apple handles information for its services under Apple’s privacy policy.

4. Support email

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond and investigate your request. Please avoid unnecessary personal or sensitive information. You can contact us to request access to or deletion of support correspondence. We retain correspondence only as needed to provide support or meet applicable obligations.

5. Retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until removed by you or the system. Deleting the app may remove local progress. Device backup availability depends on your settings and is not guaranteed by the app. Eligible purchases can be verified again using Restore Purchase; this does not restore deleted gameplay data.

6. This website

These pages do not include advertising or analytics scripts. The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP address and browser information, to deliver and protect the website. Visiting the website does not upload your game progress.

7. Changes and contact

We will update this policy if our data practices change. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to abc466254511@gmail.com.
